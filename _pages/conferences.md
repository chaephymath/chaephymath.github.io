### [CERN-Korean Summer Student Program](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1642096/)

**June 29–July 11, 2026 · CERN, Geneva, Switzerland · Participant**

Jointly presented _The Power of Symmetry: From Rotations to Particles, Forces, and Gravity_ with Drim Choi and Seokwon Lee. My part, “Massless Spin-2 and Einstein Gravity,” discussed how gauge invariance and the self-consistent coupling of a massless spin-2 field lead from Fierz–Pauli theory to Einstein gravity.
