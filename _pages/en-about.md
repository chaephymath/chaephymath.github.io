Hello! I am **Chaeun Park**, an undergraduate student at Seoul National University, majoring in Physics with a double major in Mathematical Sciences.

My interests lie broadly at the intersection of analysis, geometry, and theoretical physics. I am particularly interested in nonlinear PDEs and fluid dynamics, hyperbolic geometry and geometric group theory, and the mathematical structures underlying quantum field theory and gravitation.

This website records my studies in mathematics and physics, research interests, projects, teaching, and academic activities. As I am still learning, I hope to document not only polished conclusions but also the questions and ideas that develop along the way.
