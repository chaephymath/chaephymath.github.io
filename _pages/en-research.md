## Research Interests

### Nonlinear PDEs and Fluids

I am interested in the existence, regularity, and nonlinear dynamics of fluid equations, particularly the Euler equations, with attention to the effects of boundaries and singular geometry.

### Hyperbolic Geometry and Geometric Group Theory

I am interested in discrete group actions on hyperbolic spaces, especially Fuchsian groups, and in how algebraic and representation-theoretic properties are reflected in geometry, topology, and dynamics.

### Quantum Field Theory and Gravitation

I am interested in the relation between quantum field theory and spacetime geometry, from conserved quantities such as Komar integrals and ADM mass to holographic duality in AdS/CFT. Related interests include the connections among black-hole physics, quantum chaos, random matrix theory, and SYK models.

I am currently developing these directions through coursework and independent study. Research notes, presentations, and future results will be collected here as they become ready to share.
