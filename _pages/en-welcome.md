Hello! I am Chaeun Park, an undergraduate student studying mathematics and physics.

I created this website to restate the concepts and questions I encounter in my own words. My current interests span nonlinear PDEs and fluid dynamics; hyperbolic geometry and geometric group theory; and quantum field theory and gravitation. I plan to share course notes, independent explorations, research topics that catch my attention, and the development of future projects.

Rather than presenting only finished results, I hope this will be a place where ideas become clearer over time. The notes may occasionally be incomplete, but I will continue to revise them as I learn.

Thank you for visiting.
